Trading fees are the public cost of moving through the launch market.

The model is meant to stay readable for users while the router handles the internal distribution behind the scenes.

## What the fee covers

The trading fee supports more than one lane at the same time.

It can fund:

- creator trade earnings where active
- league funding
- recruiter and squad reward lanes
- airdrop funding lanes
- protocol revenue and treasury operations

## What users should remember

The fee is part of the trade.
It is not a hidden charge added after the quote.

The important rule is simple.
Reward systems are funded inside the active fee envelope, not stacked on top of it one by one.

## Why routing matters

Two users can pay the same public trading fee while the platform routes the proceeds differently behind the scenes.

That is how MemeWarzone can support creator, league, recruiter, and treasury lanes without turning the public fee model into a mess.

## Field order

Before you trade, confirm the campaign state, price movement, and route you are using.
Do not assume a live market is a safe market.
