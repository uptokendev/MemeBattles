UpVotes are a paid visibility action.

They are built to make promotion visible in public instead of hiding it behind rumor, private deals, or copied attention farming.

## What an UpVote does

An UpVote pushes a campaign harder through the attention lanes that matter.

That can affect featured surfaces, trending views, and competition lanes that measure public attention.

## What an UpVote does not mean

An UpVote is not:

- a safety label
- a quality guarantee
- a promise of price action
- a platform endorsement

Money spent on visibility is still only visibility.

## Why there is a fee

The fee makes the signal costly enough to matter.

If attention is free, it is easy to fake.
If attention has a cost, users can at least see that somebody chose to spend real value to push a campaign harder.

## Before you confirm

Check the campaign page.
Check the wallet prompt.
Confirm you are on the official MemeWarzone route.
Do not approve random links that claim they can UpVote for you.
