Recruiters are the growth operators inside MemeWarzone.

They bring creators and traders into the platform, maintain a squad path around real activity, and keep distribution working across launches and competition surfaces.

## What the recruiter role does

A recruiter uses a code and share path to bring users into the product.
From there, the recruiter can manage the linked growth lane through the public recruiter surface and the private dashboard tools.

## What matters most

The recruiter role is not just a referral link.
It is an operating role built around:

- onboarding
- attribution
- squad growth
- linked campaign activity
- recurring visibility and retention

## What a recruiter needs to manage

A recruiter should keep these items under control:

- one clean recruiter code
- one clean share path
- a clear public profile
- active follow through with linked users
- clean communication around official product routes

## Rule of engagement

Recruiters bring users into the platform.
They do not own those users and they do not override the platform rules for rewards, claims, or eligibility.
