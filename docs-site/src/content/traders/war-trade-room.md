title: War Trade Room
description: How the War Trade Room handles DRAFT, PRE, and POST states across chains.
---

The War Trade Room is the state aware discovery and trading surface.

Do not read every row the same way. The allowed action set changes with the state.

## DRAFT

DRAFT is the intelligence state.

Use it to read:

- campaign identity
- launch timing
- creator notes
- early interest signals
- links and community direction

Do not expect live trade actions in DRAFT.

## PRE

PRE is the bonding curve state.

This is the live trade lane before graduation. Use PRE when the campaign is open for launchpad trading and the row exposes quote, buy, sell, or related action buttons.

## POST

POST is the post graduation state.

On BNB, POST can point into the Topaz market path when that route is active for the campaign.

On Solana, POST must stay marked planned or partial until the post graduation trading lane is proven. A visible state label is not the same as a live DEX rail.

## Search and filters

Use the room like an operations table.

- filter by chain
- filter by state
- search by ticker, name, creator, token, or campaign address
- open Token Details when you need the full campaign view

## Readiness discipline

Two rules matter here.

- A visible row does not prove final settlement wiring.
- A chain switch can change what actions are available.
