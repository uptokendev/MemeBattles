Leagues are the public competition layer of MemeWarzone.

They turn launch and trading activity into standings, categories, and claimable outcomes instead of leaving everything as raw volume and noise.

## What leagues are for

Leagues give the platform a recurring structure for:

- creator competition
- trader competition
- attention based categories
- published standings
- claim based rewards after results are sealed

## What users should expect

A league is not a raffle and it is not a promise.
It is a rule set.
If a user or campaign does not meet the rules, that activity can be left out of the result.

## What leagues depend on

League outcomes depend on more than one signal.
They can depend on campaign state, activity quality, category rules, and anti abuse controls.

## Periods

Leagues can run on weekly and monthly clocks.
That gives the platform room for both short bursts of action and longer campaigns that keep performing over time.
