Leagues run in timed competition windows called epochs.

An epoch is the period where activity counts, results are sealed, and reward claims are prepared.

## Two clocks

MemeWarzone can run more than one reward clock at the same time.

The public structure is:

- weekly competition for recurring action
- monthly competition for larger standings and longer performance arcs

## What happens inside an epoch

An epoch usually moves through four steps:

1. activity is recorded
2. qualification rules are applied
3. results are published
4. claims open for eligible users

## Why epochs matter

Without a clean time boundary, reward systems become harder to audit and easier to dispute.

Epochs keep the battlefield readable.
Users know when activity counts and when settlement should follow.

## What users should not assume

Do not assume that volume alone wins an epoch.
League logic can weigh category rules, exclusions, anti abuse checks, and route specific conditions before winners are published.

## Where to verify status

Use the product surfaces and the claim flow to confirm whether an epoch is active, sealed, published, or claimable.
