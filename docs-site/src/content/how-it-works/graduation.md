title: Graduation
description: What happens when a campaign reaches the graduation threshold.
---

Graduation is the transition point between the launch market and the next trading lane.

It marks the moment when a campaign has reached the active threshold for its current route and environment.

## What changes at graduation

Graduation is the handoff.
The campaign leaves pure PRE state and moves into the next configured market context.

That process includes:

1. the threshold is reached
2. the finalize path runs
3. fee routing for the transition is applied
4. the remaining value follows the active post finalize policy
5. the campaign moves into its next trading lane

## What graduation does not prove

Graduation does not prove:

- long term strength
- honest future behavior
- prize eligibility
- low risk conditions

It proves only that the campaign crossed the configured transition line and the policy executed.

## Command note

Do not teach one old threshold or one old split as permanent law.
The live policy belongs to the current deployment state.
