title: Launch lifecycle
description: The campaign path from preparation through PRE trading, graduation, POST market activity, and rewards.
---

A MemeWarzone campaign moves through a clear sequence from preparation to market activity.

## 1. Prepare the campaign

The creator sets the campaign identity before launch.

This includes:

- name and ticker
- logo and visuals
- description
- official links
- community plan
- launch timing

Creators can use Draft when they want a staging period or Direct when the campaign is ready to move immediately.

## 2. Enter PRE

PRE is the live launchpad market state.

During PRE:

- traders can buy and sell through the active launchpad market
- the campaign gains visibility through activity and UpVotes
- league and reward systems can begin tracking eligible activity
- the campaign progresses toward graduation

## 3. Reach graduation

Graduation happens when the campaign reaches the active graduation conditions for its network.

The finalize process then applies the active fee and treasury rules and moves the campaign into its post graduation state.

Read **[Graduation](/platform/graduation)** for the current mechanics.

## 4. Enter POST

POST is the market state after graduation.

The War Trade Room and Token Details show the controls available for the campaign on its selected chain.

## 5. Continue into competition and rewards

Graduation is not the end of the campaign.

Campaigns can continue through:

- Arena activity
- league competition
- creator reputation
- reward cycles
- claims

The campaign remains part of the broader MemeWarzone battlefield after the launch phase closes.

Read **[Campaign System](/platform/campaign-lifecycle)** for the complete operating model.
