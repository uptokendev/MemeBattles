The bonding curve is the launch market before graduation.

This is the PRE phase. It is where a campaign first proves demand and where traders first meet the market.

## What the bonding curve does

The bonding curve gives every campaign a live market before post graduation routing takes over.

That matters because the campaign can build momentum, rankings, and market history before it reaches the next stage.

## What traders should expect

PRE markets can move fast.
Price impact is often sharper early.
Momentum can build quickly and reverse quickly.

Do not confuse speed with safety.

## What creators should focus on

This phase is where attention turns into visible market action.

A creator should keep the page clean, the links official, and the message disciplined.
If the campaign is unclear here, it gets punished here.

## Why the bonding curve matters to the platform

The bonding curve is not only a price mechanism.
It is also where discovery, fee routing, league tracking, and early campaign credibility begin to take shape.
