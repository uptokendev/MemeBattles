## 1. What this statement covers

This statement applies to information processed by MemeWarzone in connection with:

- browsing the website or docs site
- connecting a wallet
- creating or interacting with campaigns
- trading through the platform interface
- using UpVotes, comments, chats, profiles, leaderboards, and dashboards
- joining recruiter, squad, league, airdrop, or reward systems
- contacting support or participating in community operations

Public blockchains are separate from MemeWarzone. Blockchain transactions are public, permanent, and controlled by the relevant network, not by MemeWarzone.

## 2. Information we may collect

### Wallet and blockchain information

When you connect a wallet or interact with the platform, we may process:

- wallet address
- chain ID and network information
- wallet signatures used for authentication or authorization
- transaction hashes
- campaign interactions
- UpVote activity
- trade, graduation, reward, and claim-related events
- public blockchain data needed to display balances, positions, activity, and eligibility

### Account, profile, and content information

If you create or edit a profile, campaign, recruiter profile, squad profile, or other public content, we may process:

- display name, username, avatar, bio, and social links
- campaign name, ticker, description, media, links, and updates
- comments, chat messages, support messages, and other user-submitted content
- recruiter code, attribution status, squad membership, leaderboard identity, and public profile details

### Contact and support information

If you provide contact details or communicate with us, we may process:

- email address
- Telegram, Discord, X, or other social handles you provide
- support messages and moderation reports
- communications about claims, safety, incidents, or account issues

### Technical and usage information

When you use the site or app, we may process technical information such as:

- IP address
- device and browser information
- approximate location derived from IP address
- pages viewed and actions taken
- timestamps, referrers, logs, error reports, and diagnostic data
- cookies, local storage, and similar technologies

### Rewards, eligibility, and anti-abuse information

To operate leagues, recruiter rewards, squad rewards, airdrops, claims, ranking, and anti-abuse systems, we may process:

- wallet-to-recruiter attribution
- squad membership and detachment status
- activity scores and eligibility status
- pending, claimable, claimed, expired, or excluded rewards
- broad ineligibility reason codes shown to users
- internal risk signals, clustering outputs, review queues, and abuse-detection records

Some anti-abuse details are kept internal so the system cannot be farmed or manipulated.

## 3. How we use information

We use information to:

- provide, maintain, and improve the website, docs, app, and APIs
- authenticate wallets and sessions
- display campaigns, profiles, comments, leaderboards, charts, activity feeds, and dashboards
- process UpVotes, campaign actions, reward eligibility, claims, and public winner pages
- operate recruiter, squad, league, and airdrop systems
- detect spam, bots, Sybil behavior, wash trading, self-trading, circular trading, fake demand, and other abuse
- protect users, campaigns, protocol funds, reward pools, and platform integrity
- debug errors, monitor uptime, improve performance, and secure infrastructure
- communicate with users about support, safety, legal, operational, or product updates
- comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Service

## 4. Legal bases for processing

Where applicable privacy laws require a legal basis, we process personal data under one or more of the following bases:

- **Contract:** to provide the service you request, including wallet login, campaign actions, dashboards, claims, and support.
- **Legitimate interests:** to operate, secure, improve, and protect the platform, including analytics, fraud prevention, abuse detection, and public transparency features.
- **Consent:** where we ask for consent, such as optional communications or certain cookies where required.
- **Legal obligation:** where processing is needed to comply with laws, lawful requests, tax/accounting requirements, sanctions screening, or dispute handling.

## 5. Public information and blockchain transparency

MemeWarzone is built around public competition and transparent reward flows. Public surfaces may include:

- campaign pages
- public wallet or profile activity connected to platform actions
- UpVote activity
- league standings and winners
- recruiter profiles and recruiter leaderboards
- squad leaderboards and public member ranking
- Warzone BNB Airdrop winner pages
- broad reward rules and broad anti-abuse categories

Blockchain data is public by design. We cannot delete, hide, or reverse information that has already been written to a public blockchain.

## 6. How we share information

We may share information with:

- infrastructure providers that host, store, secure, or process platform data
- database, storage, API, realtime, analytics, security, logging, and monitoring providers
- wallet, blockchain, RPC, indexer, and transaction infrastructure providers
- moderation, support, legal, accounting, audit, and compliance providers
- authorities, regulators, courts, or law enforcement where legally required or necessary to protect rights and safety
- successor entities in connection with a merger, acquisition, restructuring, or asset transfer

We do not sell personal information for money. If future advertising, analytics, or data-sharing practices create additional opt-out rights under applicable law, we will update this statement and provide the required controls.

## 7. Cookies and local storage

We may use cookies, local storage, and similar technologies to:

- keep the site working
- remember preferences
- maintain sessions
- improve performance
- understand usage patterns
- protect against abuse

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Blocking some cookies or local storage may break wallet connection, session, dashboard, or security features.

## 8. Data retention

We keep information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this statement, including operating the platform, resolving disputes, enforcing rules, maintaining security, satisfying legal obligations, and preserving accurate reward and abuse-prevention records.

Some records may be kept longer where needed for security, anti-abuse, legal, accounting, audit, or dispute reasons. Public blockchain data may remain available permanently on the relevant network.

## 9. Security

We use technical and organizational measures designed to protect information, including access controls, infrastructure security, monitoring, and separation of public, user-private, and admin-only data.

No system is perfectly secure. Users are responsible for securing their own wallets, private keys, seed phrases, devices, and accounts. MemeWarzone will never ask for your seed phrase or private key.

## 10. International transfers

Our providers and infrastructure may process information in different countries. Where applicable law requires safeguards for international transfers, we will use appropriate transfer mechanisms or rely on legally recognized transfer bases.

## 11. Your privacy rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to:

- access personal data we hold about you
- correct inaccurate personal data
- delete personal data, subject to legal, security, anti-abuse, and blockchain limitations
- restrict or object to certain processing
- receive a portable copy of certain data
- withdraw consent where processing is based on consent
- opt out of certain sale, sharing, or targeted advertising practices where applicable
- lodge a complaint with a data protection authority

To exercise rights, contact us using the contact details above. We may need to verify that you control the wallet or account connected to the request.

## 12. Children

MemeWarzone is not intended for children. You must not use the platform if you are under the minimum age required by the Terms of Service or by the laws that apply to you.
