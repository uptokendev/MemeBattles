Security on MemeWarzone is built around one hard truth.

The platform can improve structure, visibility, and recovery discipline.
It cannot remove speculation risk from the market.

## What the security model tries to do

The goal is to reduce blind trust and force more of the critical information into visible product surfaces.

That includes:

- clearer launch state
- visible route and reward behavior
- separation between reward routing and operating funds
- warnings around fake links and unsafe assumptions
- controls that can pause or restrict risky paths

## What users must still do

Users still need to verify wallets, links, campaign context, and transaction prompts.

No platform surface can save a user who signs the wrong transaction in the wrong place.

## What not to assume

Do not assume that any of these signals mean a token is safe:

- an UpVote
- a featured placement
- a rising chart
- a crowded page
- a claim notice from an unofficial link

## Why this matters

Speculative systems fail fast when users are trained to trust noise.
MemeWarzone is trying to make the noise legible, not to pretend it does not exist.
