Warzone BNB Airdrops are the weekly reward lane for active smaller users.

The goal is to keep the field open to more than the largest wallets by giving disciplined participation its own route to compete.

![Warzone airdrops status board](/images/docs/warzone-airdrops-status.png)

## What the program does

Warzone BNB Airdrops can reward eligible trader activity and eligible creator activity during the active weekly epoch.

## What matters most

Users should remember four points:

- the program runs weekly
- qualification still depends on the published rules
- winner selection is not just a raw biggest wallet list
- rewards move through the claim flow after publication

## What the public manual should tell you

The public manual explains the purpose of the program, the broad qualification lane, and where to check your status.
It does not publish private abuse thresholds or internal review logic.

## Where to check your lane

Use the airdrop pages for the rule set.
Use Command Center and the reward dashboard for your own status and claim state.
