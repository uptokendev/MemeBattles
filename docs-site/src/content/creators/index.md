Creators open the battlefield.

A strong creator does more than deploy a token. They prepare the page, line up the message, launch cleanly, rally the audience, and keep attention alive long enough for the campaign to matter.

## Creator mission

The creator path is simple:

1. choose Direct or Draft
2. prepare the campaign page and links
3. launch through the correct wallet and chain
4. build visibility through real community activity
5. move through PRE and later market states as the campaign grows

## What good creators do

Strong creators keep four things clear:

- one clean message
- one clean set of official links
- one clear launch plan
- one clear reason for people to care

## What creators gain

Creators can gain:

- live market participation around their campaign
- public visibility through discovery and competition surfaces
- stronger reputation for future launches
- direct creator earnings where active under the live policy
